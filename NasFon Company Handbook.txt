Introduction
Welcome to NasFon.
NasFon is a software development company that designs, builds, and scales innovative digital solutions for businesses, startups, organizations, and individuals.
We believe technology should solve meaningful problems, empower people, and create lasting value. Every product we build reflects our commitment to quality, innovation, transparency, and long-term partnerships.
This handbook defines who we are, what we believe, how we work, and the standards that guide every decision we make. It serves as the foundation for our culture, our products, and our relationships with clients, partners, and one another.

Company Definition
NasFon is a software development company that designs, builds, and scales innovative digital solutions for businesses, startups, organizations, and individuals.
Beyond software development, we serve as a long-term technology partner, helping clients transform ideas into reliable, scalable, and impactful digital products. From product strategy and design to engineering, deployment, and continuous improvement, we work alongside our clients to build technology that creates lasting value.

Vision
To become one of the world's most trusted software companies, empowering businesses, entrepreneurs, developers, and organizations to transform ideas into impactful digital products that improve lives.

Mission
NasFon builds reliable, scalable, and high-quality software solutions while partnering with clients and innovators to transform ideas into successful digital products.
Through technical excellence, strategic thinking, and long-term collaboration, we help our partners achieve meaningful and lasting outcomes.

Decision Filter
Before making important decisions, ask:
Does this create value for the customer?
Does this align with our mission and values?
Is it secure, reliable, and scalable?
Is it simple and maintainable?
Will it strengthen long-term trust?
Will we be proud of this decision in five years?
If the answer is yes, we move forward.
If not, we rethink the decision.
